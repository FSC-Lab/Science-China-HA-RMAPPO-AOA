HA-RMAPPO · two stages and three team sizes

Each configuration runs 5 consecutive predetermined-seed episodes, retaining every success, breach, timeout and unresolved outcome. Verified success requires the native success flag, capture of every intruder within the capture radius, and no sticky, sampled-position or interpolated-segment breach. Capture distances and CSV terminal states are checked independently. Native callback boundary inconsistencies remain unverified. These 30 fixed-checkpoint episodes illustrate policy behavior and are not an estimate of the paper's defense success rate (DSR).
